A rehabilitation-informed approach

What does that mean in my practice?

I use what I learned through the IWI MES Trainer qualification to better understand movement patterns and plan gradual increases in training load.

This can be particularly helpful after a long break, when returning after an injury, when repetitive demands have affected your confidence, or when you simply want to move more confidently again.

The aim is to build stable foundations for long-term strength, muscle and endurance, as well as helping you through today’s session.

Professional boundaries

Training with clear professional boundaries.

I don’t diagnose conditions, and personal training does not replace medical care or physiotherapy. Acute pain, a recent injury or unexplained symptoms need assessment by an appropriate healthcare professional first.

If you already have guidance from a doctor or physiotherapist, we can plan your return to training within those recommendations.

Who is it for?

You’re just starting
