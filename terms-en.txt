2. Scope of these terms

These terms describe the framework for personal training, online coaching and the standalone training programme. The individual offer identifies the selected service and its details. Neither these terms nor an individual agreement may restrict mandatory consumer rights.

The website is for enquiries and appointment scheduling. It has no online payment or automatic ordering process that creates an obligation to pay. Submitting the form or selecting a Calendly appointment does not, by itself, create a paid training contract or a cancellation charge.

Under the intended process, online contracting is used for online coaching. Gym clients sign a separate paper training agreement by hand, in person, before training begins and receive a copy. The website does not replace that agreement.

The standalone Training Program is available through a separate offer. If it or any other service is actually sold through a distance agreement, such as an email exchange, the applicable distance-contract rules still apply. Legal classification depends on how the contract is actually concluded; a later paper signature or the payment method does not override this.

A minor may participate only with the involvement of their legal representative required by law and with suitable, age-appropriate arrangements agreed in advance.

6. Cancelling or changing appointments

The 24-hour rule applies only to appointments booked under an existing paid contract. Cancelling the free consultation is free of charge. Deadlines are measured in elapsed hours using the time zone shown in the booking confirmation.

The Client may cancel through the cancellation option in the confirmation email, the agreed messaging channel or the Provider's email address. The time of notification counts; the Client does not need to wait for Bence's reply.

If the Client cancels less than 24 hours before the appointment, the fee agreed in advance for that appointment is normally non-refundable. For a ten-session personal training package, one session is used. Cancelling one online coaching call does not automatically forfeit the entire monthly package: at most, the affected, previously identified call is not replaced; no new penalty fee is added.

The arrangements for cancellations at least 24 hours in advance and rescheduling must be clarified before this draft is finalised. Bence may make exceptions in the Client's favour where circumstances justify them.

If Bence cancels, the Client may choose a replacement appointment or a refund for the affected, unperformed session. A session missed due to Bence cannot be deducted from a package.

This rule does not restrict statutory withdrawal or termination rights, remedies for defective performance, or the 24-hour reply guarantee. It cannot justify an unfair or disproportionate charge.

10. Consumer withdrawal and termination

For a distance service contract, a consumer normally has 14 days from conclusion of the contract to withdraw without giving a reason. If performance has already begun at the consumer's express request, they may terminate without giving a reason within the remaining period. Failure to provide withdrawal information may extend the statutory deadline.

Starting within the 14-day period requires a separate express request; it is not inferred from accepting these terms or booking a slot. The Client must acknowledge that the right is lost once the service has been fully performed. For partial performance, a proportionate fee for services actually supplied may be charged only where the necessary information and request for early performance exist. Otherwise, the statutory exemption from those costs applies.

Early supply of digital content has separate conditions for loss of the right: prior express consent, express acknowledgement of that loss, and the required confirmation. A personalised programme or delivery of a file does not automatically remove withdrawal rights. The digital-download exception is not automatically applied to an entire coaching service.

To meet the deadline, it is sufficient to send a clear withdrawal or termination notice before it expires. The model below is optional. Notice may be sent by email or post using the contact details in section 1. Keeping evidence of sending is recommended. Receipt of the notice is acknowledged without undue delay.

Any amount due is refunded no later than 14 days after notice of withdrawal is received, normally using the original payment method. A different method requires the Client's express agreement and must not create extra costs for them. Any lawful proportionate performance charge is transparently accounted for.

Online withdrawal function: where applicable, the electronic withdrawal function required by section 22 of Hungarian Government Decree 45/2014 must be provided in the contracting process. The current website's email-draft and Calendly cancellation options are not that function. The necessary function and durable acknowledgement remain to be implemented, which is why this document is a review draft.

A training agreement concluded in person at the Provider's usual business premises does not automatically carry the 14-day distance-contract right. Off-premises or actual distance agreements remain subject to their applicable rules. The exception for leisure services on specific dates is not used as a blanket exclusion covering every package.
