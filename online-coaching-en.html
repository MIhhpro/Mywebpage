Could this be for you?

Train independently. Have support within reach.

It could be a good fit if…

  • You live elsewhere or train at different times each week.
  • You exercise independently and are happy to keep a training log.
  • You’ll tell me when something feels difficult, is unclear or doesn’t fit your week.
  • You’d like feedback on your technique and progress.

If you’d rather have me there…

It’s completely fine if you’d feel more comfortable with someone beside you for each exercise. Personal training may be a better starting point.

In practice

Your plan develops with your training.

  1. 01

    I get to know your starting point

    We discuss your training history, goals, available equipment and how much time you have in a typical week.

  2. 02

    We agree on the details

    Choose the package that suits you. Basic includes one coaching call per week; Plus and Premium include two. We’ll agree on call times before starting.

  3. 03

    You train and record your experience

    Alongside weights and repetitions, note what felt easy, where you struggled and how you felt.

  4. 04

    I give feedback, and we adjust

    We discuss your training during coaching calls and update the programme. Premium also includes video form checks, unlimited messaging and ongoing adjustments. The standalone Training Program includes the four-week plan.

Before you start

A few more things you might be wondering.

Are these live video workouts?+

You complete your workouts independently. Basic includes one coaching call per week, and Plus and Premium include two, to discuss your progress. Video form checks are part of Premium.

Can I train at home?+

Tell me how much space and what equipment you have. We’ll see whether we can build a home programme that suits your goals.

How is a training programme different from coaching?+

For a one-off HUF 19,900, the Training Program gives you a personalised four-week plan. Monthly coaching also includes regular calls and programme adjustments: Basic HUF 29,900 / month, Plus HUF 39,900 / month, Premium HUF 49,900 / month. Unlimited messaging and video form checks are included in Premium.

What if I miss a workout?+

With coaching, we’ll discuss what changed at the next call and adjust the programme. Premium also lets us check in by message between calls. The standalone Training Program does not include coaching calls or ongoing programme adjustments.
