Your first session

You don’t have to know
how to begin.

That’s why we’re meeting. It isn’t a test, and you don’t need to get in shape beforehand. I want to understand where you are now and what would help you get started.

Step by step

First we talk. Then we plan.

  1. 01

    Choose a time

    Select the free introductory consultation on the contact page. Your booking is complete once you confirm it in the calendar; the details arrive by email.

  2. 02

    Tell me what brought you here

    You might have a specific goal, or simply want to move more regularly. We’ll talk about your experience and what could fit into your everyday life.

  3. 03

    We discuss the next steps

    We’ll explore personal training and online coaching, prices and how we could work together. After the free conversation, you decide whether to continue.

  4. 04

    We build the basics in your first workout

    If we continue together, we’ll get to know how you move and practise the first exercises. I’ll explain what to focus on, and we’ll plan your next session.

What should you prepare?

A few small things to help you arrive at ease.

For the conversation

Think about what you’d like to change, your experience of exercise and when you could make time for it regularly. You don’t need a polished answer.

For your first workout

Bring comfortable sportswear, clean indoor trainers, a towel and water. We’ll confirm where to meet and the gym’s admission arrangements before you arrive.

Feel free to ask

You’re not the only one wondering.

I’ve never been to a gym. Is that a problem?+

Not at all. In your first session, we’ll get familiar with the exercises and equipment. Ask whenever you’d like another demonstration or a different explanation.

Is the free consultation a free workout?+

The consultation is an introductory conversation. Personal training is a separate service, currently priced at HUF 11,000 per session, or HUF 100,000 for ten sessions.

What about gym admission and package terms?+

Before starting, we’ll clarify gym admission, session lengths, package validity and cancellation terms so you have the details you need to decide.

I don’t know what my goal should be. Can I still get in touch?+

Yes. We can start with why you want to move more or where you’d like to feel more confident. Together, we’ll turn that into a goal we can track.
