Before you decide

A few things to know first.

I don’t know which option to choose.+

Start with a free consultation. We’ll consider your schedule, experience and goals to find the support that would help you most.

Where does personal training take place?+

At Victory Fitness Békásmegyer: 1039 Budapest, Hatvany Lajos utca 10. Location and preparing for your first session →

Can I join as a beginner?+

Yes. You don’t need any experience or a particular fitness level. We’ll start with the basics and get familiar with the equipment.

What does the price include?+

Personal training costs HUF 11,000 per session, or HUF 100,000 for ten sessions. The four-week Training Program has a one-off fee of HUF 19,900. Monthly online coaching costs HUF 29,900 for Basic, HUF 39,900 for Plus and HUF 49,900 for Premium. We’ll clarify gym admission, session lengths and the exact package terms before you start.

When will I notice progress?+

At the start, we’ll decide what to track: your technique, repetitions or consistency, for example. There’s no universal deadline. We measure change against your own starting point.

Let’s first talk about what would help you. The consultation is free and doesn’t commit you to further sessions.
