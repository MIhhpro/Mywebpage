I remember believing I would never manage my first pull-up.

That’s why I want you to feel comfortable asking questions, making mistakes and trying again. I’ll show you the movement, explain why we’re doing it and help you find the right level of challenge for today.

I’ve trained since September 2017. Alongside that experience, I’ve earned three IWI qualifications, and I’m still learning. I want you to understand your own training better as we go.
